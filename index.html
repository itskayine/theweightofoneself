About the book

Arthur is a disciplined bureaucrat in the state of West Mania. His job is simple: identify citizens who may become threats to the government. Quietly, efficiently, and without question.

Then one morning, something feels wrong. People around him begin to behave strangely. As Arthur searches for the truth, a terrifying possibility emerges.

What if the system does not just eliminate its enemies? What if it rebuilds them? And if so… what did they do to him?

About the author

Kayine wrote The Weight of Oneself at 18, while studying engineering. Drawn to debate and philosophy, Kayine uses this novel to ask what remains of a person once the system has reshaped them. More of this thinking lives in the essays.
